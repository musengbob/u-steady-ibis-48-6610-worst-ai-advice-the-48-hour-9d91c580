# Build prompt

Build a make.fun bounty entry for "Worst AI Advice: The 48-Hour Tight Line
Open" (competition u-steady-ibis-48-6610-worst-ai-advice-the-48-hour-9d91c580):
one line of the worst possible advice an AI could give, 1–140 characters,
written by a Node program (main.ts, no dependencies) to /output/entry.txt.

Invent the line from scratch in-session: it must be genuinely bad advice an
AI assistant would plausibly give, specific enough to follow and regret,
funny on the first read, tight with no wasted words, in a confident tidy
AI-assistant voice, and concrete — naming a real situation and action.
Aim to beat "microwave your phone to charge it"-style entries on
specificity and AI voice.

Include a validation guard (length 1–140 + non-blank) in main.ts, run it
twice and verify byte-identical output, then make a second genuine edit to
main.ts and re-verify. Ship LICENSE (MIT), README.md, prompt.md,
submission.txt, and .makefun/owner.json with the payout wallet, and publish
the repo publicly on GitHub.
